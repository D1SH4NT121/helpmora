# Privacy Notice

**Project:** HELPmora  
**Repository:** [https://github.com/D1SH4NT121/helpmora](https://github.com/D1SH4NT121/helpmora)  
**Last updated:** October 2026

HELPmora helps individuals find social welfare assistance across housing, food security, healthcare, and legal aid. Many people who use this system are experiencing acute distress — domestic violence, unlawful eviction, hunger, or financial crises. This privacy notice explains plainly how your information is handled.

HELPmora is an open-source civic technology platform. It is **not** a government portal, law firm, or benefits distributor, and using it does not submit legal applications on your behalf.

---

## Key Privacy Protections

1. **No Account or Real Name Required:**
   - There is no registration, email sign-up, or phone number requirement.
   - You can use the navigator completely anonymously. We never ask for your name, Aadhaar number, PAN, bank account password, or home address.

2. **Your Messages Are Never Stored or Sold:**
   - The engine parses your message on the fly to determine eligibility parameters (e.g., household size, approximate income, need category).
   - The raw message text is never logged to server disk, stored in external databases, or sold to third parties.

3. **Zero Mandatory External AI Calls:**
   - Core eligibility decisions, scoring formulas, and emergency routings are evaluated by **local deterministic code**.
   - Your personal situation details are not sent to third-party commercial LLM providers on the critical evaluation path.

4. **Instant Crisis Handling:**
   - When messages indicate acute danger, violence, or mental health crises, the system routes directly to official, toll-free Indian support helplines:
     - **112**: Emergency Services
     - **181**: Women Helpline (24/7 crisis response)
     - **1098**: Childline (Child protection & rescue)
     - **14416**: Tele-MANAS (Mental health support)

5. **Quick Exit for Shared Devices:**
   - If you are on a shared or monitored phone/computer and need to leave immediately, click the **Quick Exit** button (or press Shift three times).
   - This instantly closes the session and navigates to a neutral page.

6. **Delete My Data:**
   - At any time, you can clear all active state by clicking the "New case" button or resetting your browser session storage.
